# Belgian Dark Ale on a Roasted Grain Bill

**A malt-forward Belgian dark ale built on a deep roasted multi-malt base with abbey yeast character.
The grain bill borrows from the stout tradition — layered dark malts, full body, dry roast — but the fermentation character and overall expression are squarely Belgian.**

Dense and dark with espresso, bitter chocolate, dried fruit, and a soft mandarin accent from late hops and whirlpool.
BE-256 drives the beer: warm esters pull the roast toward dark fruit rather than coal and ash.

**~5.0% ABV · ~12 IBU · ~90 EBC · serve at 7–10°C**

## Notes

- Roasted multi-malt base intentionally layered: Teak for body and plum notes, Chokolade for bittersweet cocoa, Ebony for dry roast, Black for color depth without harsh astringency
- BE-256 selected for warm ester profile — bridges dark roast with citrus hop character
- Mandarina Bavaria used as a soft accent, not a dominant hop component
- No dry hop
- Low measured IBU compensated by roast bitterness from dark malts
- Two-sachet pitch for reliable attenuation at lower gravity

---

## Flavor Profile

- espresso and bitter chocolate
- dark dried fruit (prune, raisin)
- warm Belgian fermentation esters
- soft mandarin and tangerine peel in the finish
- dry roast on the back end
- medium-full body, smooth finish

---

## Style Notes

A Belgian dark ale, not a stout. The grain bill borrows stout-tradition malts for depth and color, but the yeast defines the beer.

BE-256 pulls the roast character toward dark fruit and warmth rather than coal and ash. The citrus-roast pairing is unconventional and intentional.

Not intended as a BJCP clone of Belgian Dubbel or Quadrupel — darker, drier, and more roast-forward than either.
