# Wheat Ale with Mandarina Accent

**A straightforward malt-forward wheat ale built on a substantial wheat base with restrained modern hop expression.
WB-06 at 19°C gives moderate banana and soft spice without the caricature — a clean, drinkable hefeweizen-style beer with a citrus lift from late hops.**

Hazy, pale, and aromatic. Soft banana and clove from fermentation, mandarin and tangerine from the whirlpool, rounded wheat body.

**~4.7% ABV · ~8 IBU · ~8 EBC · serve at 5–8°C**

## Boil & Hops

Boil: **90 minutes**

| Addition | Timing | Hop | Amount | AA% |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Flavor/Bittering | 45 min before end | Mandarina Bavaria | 15 g | 7.2% |
| Whirlpool | 80°C, 15 min | Mandarina Bavaria | 35 g | 7.2% |

Whirlpool held at 80°C — lower than dark ale recipes to preserve aromatic oils and minimize additional isomerization.

## Notes

- WB-06 fermented at 19°C — moderate banana expression, not dominant; soft clove in background
- Whirlpool at 80°C rather than 90°C — preserves hop aromatics, keeps bitterness contribution minimal
- No dry hop
- Shorter conditioning cycle than dark ale variants — wheat beer benefits from drinking fresh
- Hazy by design — no fining, no filtration

---

## Flavor Profile

- soft banana and wheat bread
- subtle clove and spice from WB-06
- mandarin and tangerine from whirlpool
- light, rounded malt body
- low bitterness, refreshing finish

---

## Style Notes

Hefeweizen-inspired but not a strict BJCP clone. The Mandarina Bavaria addition shifts it toward a modern wheat ale without abandoning the classic fermentation character.

Designed to be approachable and sessionable.
