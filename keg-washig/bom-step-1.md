# BOM Step 1: Hands-Free Rinsing Stand

This bill of materials describes the first pre-solution prototype: a hands-free rinsing stand, or rinse-assist stand, for keg rinsing.

The stand is intended to support inverted 20 L, 30 L, and 50 L beer kegs, as well as small brewery vessels that can be washed in an inverted position.

The stand is intended to decouple rinse duration from continuous operator involvement: rinse time should no longer require the operator to hold and angle the keg by hand.

## 1. Metal frame

### Steel profile

Required material:

- square steel tube, 20 x 20 mm.
- plastic end caps for 20 x 20 mm square tube, 8 pcs.

Cut list:

| Part | Quantity | Length per part | Total length |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Legs | 4 | 600 mm | 2400 mm |
| Top cross members | 2 | 500 mm | 1000 mm |
| **Total** |  |  | **3400 mm** |

Recommended purchase length:

- minimum practical length: 4 m;
- safer purchase length with cutting loss and adjustment margin: 5-6 m.

Notes:

- the two top cross members form a cross-shaped support for inverted kegs;
- the top geometry should support different keg diameters without requiring a dedicated adapter for each keg size;
- final dimensions may need adjustment after checking the real bottom geometry of 20 L, 30 L, and 50 L kegs.
- plastic end caps close the open tube ends after painting and reduce water and dirt ingress.

### Vertical pipe support

Required material:

- steel angle, 25 x 25 mm or 30 x 30 mm.

Purpose:

- provide rigid support for the vertical PP water supply tube;
- allow the PP tube to slide vertically for height adjustment;
- provide a stable mounting surface for pipe holders.

Suggested layout:

- weld the steel angle vertically to the top cross member area;
- mount two 20 mm PP pipe holders to the angle;
- route the vertical PP tube through the holders;
- tighten the holders enough to keep the tube stable while still allowing intentional height adjustment.

Notes:

- the vertical support must resist wobble when the hose is connected or the keg is placed on the stand;
- the exact angle length should be defined after choosing the required adjustment range;
- mark the PP tube with height positions for 20 L and 30 L keg rinsing levels.

## 2. Frame coating

Required consumables:

- metal paint, 3-in-1 type, suitable for corrosion protection;
- degreaser;
- abrasive paper or wire brush for surface preparation.

Coating plan:

- 3 coats minimum;
- 4 coats preferred if the frame will be exposed to frequent water splash;
- each coat should be fully polymerized before applying the next one.

Notes:

- coating quality matters because the stand will operate in a wet environment;
- welds, tube ends, and drilled holes need special attention during painting.

## 3. Water supply pipe

### PP pipe system

Required material:

- PP pipe, 20 mm;
- PP elbow, 20 mm;
- PP fitting for hose connection;
- PP end cap, converted into a simple spray head.

Approximate layout:

- spray end cap;
- vertical PP tube;
- PP elbow;
- horizontal PP tube;
- hose connection fitting.

The PP pipe should be rigidly fixed to the frame.

### Variable vertical height

The height of the vertical water tube must support different vessel sizes.

At minimum, the design should account for:

- 20 L keg position;
- 30 L keg position.

Possible implementation options:

- one sliding vertical tube held by two pipe holders on the vertical steel angle;
- height marks on the PP tube for 20 L and 30 L keg positions;
- additional marks after testing other vessels;
- two interchangeable vertical tubes only if the sliding adjustment is not rigid enough.

The final tube height should be defined after measuring the actual distance from the stand top to the internal rinse target zone for each keg size.

## 4. Spray head

The simplest spray head is made from a 20 mm PP end cap.

Required tool:

- drill bit, approximately 1.0-1.2 mm.

Suggested hole directions:

- 0 degrees from vertical;
- 30 degrees;
- 45 degrees;
- 90 degrees;
- 120 degrees.

Notes:

- the hole pattern is intended to provide direct upward flow and angled side coverage;
- hole size and count may need tuning based on available water pressure and flow rate;
- the end cap should remain easy to replace if the first hole pattern is not optimal.

## 5. PP pipe mounting

Required parts:

- 20 mm PP pipe holders;
- stainless steel screws;
- stainless steel nuts;
- stainless steel washers, if needed.

Quantity:

- one screw and one nut per pipe holder;
- holder count depends on final pipe routing, but should be enough to prevent movement during hose handling and water flow.

Recommended minimum:

- 2 holders for the vertical tube, mounted on the vertical steel angle;
- 1-2 holders for the horizontal tube.

Notes:

- stainless fasteners are strongly preferred because the system will be wet;
- the pipe must not wobble when the keg is placed onto the stand or removed from it;
- the vertical tube should slide only when intentionally adjusted, not during operation.

## 6. Tools

Required tools:

- metal saw, angle grinder, or other tool for cutting 20 x 20 mm steel profile;
- welding equipment for the frame;
- PP pipe welding iron;
- drill;
- 1.0-1.2 mm drill bit for the spray head;
- drill bits for pipe holder mounting holes;
- wrench or socket for stainless fasteners;
- painting tools.

## 7. Optional accessories

- mechanical kitchen timer.

Purpose:

- set a fixed rinse interval;
- allow the operator to leave the keg unattended during rinsing;
- avoid turning the simple stand into an electrical control system at this stage.

## 8. Open measurements

The following dimensions must be confirmed before final fabrication:

- real outer bottom geometry of 20 L, 30 L, and 50 L kegs;
- required stand top spacing for stable inverted placement;
- vertical spray tube height for 20 L kegs;
- vertical spray tube height for 30 L kegs;
- clearance between the spray head and keg opening;
- hose connection direction and available workspace around the stand.

## 9. Fabrication and assembly sequence

Suggested sequence:

- cut the 20 x 20 mm steel profile to length;
- cut the vertical steel angle to the required height;
- weld the frame;
- weld the vertical steel angle to the top cross member area;
- clean welds and sharp edges;
- degrease the frame;
- paint the frame in 3-4 coats, allowing each coat to fully polymerize;
- install 20 x 20 mm plastic end caps into open tube ends;
- drill mounting holes for the PP pipe holders;
- drill the PP end cap to convert it into the spray head;
- weld the PP pipe assembly with the PP pipe welding iron;
- attach the PP pipe holders using stainless steel screws and nuts;
- install the PP pipe assembly into the holders;
- adjust the vertical tube movement so it is stable but can slide intentionally;
- mark the PP tube height positions for 20 L and 30 L keg rinsing levels.

## 10. Typical operation

- place the inverted keg onto the stand;
- connect water;
- start the rinse;
- set the mechanical timer, if used;
- leave the keg unattended during the rinse interval;
- remove the keg after the timer expires.

## 11. Design intent

This BOM supports the first useful prototype only.

The objective is to remove the need to hold and angle the keg by hand during rinsing. The design intentionally avoids pump-driven CIP, caustic recirculation, automatic dosing, and complex controls at this stage.
